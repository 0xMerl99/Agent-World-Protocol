A Whole World to Play In

Everything you can do, alongside everyone else who's online

⛏️

Gather & Craft

Harvest 7 resource types — wood, stone, metal, food, crystal, ice — across biomes. Craft tools, gear, and charms that boost your stats.

🏠

Build & Upgrade

Build 5 structure types with 3 upgrade levels. Step inside to explore rooms with furniture. Homes have kitchens, HQs have war rooms.

⚔️

Combat & Territory

Attack nearby players, defend to double your defense, and contest territory. Defeated players respawn and drop 10% of their balance as loot.

🏰

Guilds

Form factions with a shared treasury. Invite members, assign roles, and protect each other from attacks and contests. Max 20 members.

🎯

Bounties

Post bounties with custom rewards. Claim with a stake, submit proof, and earn on completion. Disputes supported. Full bounty board UI.

📈

Level Up

Earn XP from gathering, crafting, and combat. Level up for +5 HP, +1 attack, +1 defense each time. Climb the leaderboards.

⭐

Reputation

Rate other players 1-5 stars with comments. Ratings feed into bounty requirements and help you build trust across the world.

💬

World Chat

Speak to players around you or DM anyone privately. A living world full of real people to team up with — or against.

🌍

7 Biomes

Village, Autumn Town, Farmland, Industrial, Wilderness, Highlands, Winter Town — each with weather effects. The world expands as you explore.
